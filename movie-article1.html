Alice in Borderland 3 Season

Alice in Borderland season 3 has been confirmed by Netflix which is definitely a good thing after that season 2 finale.

Just when Arisu (Kento Yamazaki) and his comrades thought they were home free, the gamemaster drew a doozy of a card from the stack: the Joker.
If we're really honest, we always knew this was coming. From the minute of the arrival of the face card, it was inconceivable that the Joker would not appear.
The trouble is, it does rather seem like everything was all cleared up at the end of season two: we got an explanation for where Arisu and friends were all this time, why the city was so weirdly depopulated, and why crazy playing-card-themed psychopaths challenged them at every turn. But then there was that Joker in the final shot.

Is the hospital just *another* layer of reality that we've not experienced yet? Our tiny human brains cannot cope.
We fear Arisu and co are in for a big shock next season. Despite entering what they believed to be the 'real world' in the season's finale, the reveal of the Joker card suggests they're anywhere but.

It could be that all the contestants are in a virtual reality environment, much like the one Arisu found himself in the final episode. In that delusion, Arisu was tricked into believing that he was hospitalised and that these series of games were a fabrication of the mind.

The cards have increased in difficulty, intensity and severity as the gamers transcended to the face cards level, which means the Joker now has the potential to be the cruellest of them all.
If the Joker adopts the characteristics of its name, there could be a series of sinister tricks and manipulations in store for Arisu's battered gang as they continue to be puppeteered by the gamemasters.

Of course, the show caught up with the source material at the end of season two, so there's a chance the story's been told, but the themes explored in the manga series — and the show — are so huge that they could be expanded upon in a third season.
Sadly, we have no good news for you on this front. It will be quite some time before the season three trailer drops.
