About Nanami Kento


Check out our first reader's little essay about their favourite character.

General

Nanami Kento is a prominent character in the popular manga and anime series "Jujutsu Kaisen." He is introduced as a Jujutsu Sorcerer and a former salaryman who left his mundane job to join the Jujutsu Tech, an organization dedicated to fighting curses and curses users. Nanami is known for his strong sense of duty, stoic personality, and exceptional combat abilities.


Skills

One of Nanami's defining traits is his pragmatic and no-nonsense approach to jujutsu battles. He values efficiency and strategy above all else and often tries to minimize collateral damage during fights.
His combat skills are top-notch, and he employs a unique jujutsu technique called the "Ratio Technique," which allows him to maximize the impact of his attacks by precisely measuring the cursed energy he uses.


Despite his gruff exterior, Nanami shows a deep sense of responsibility for protecting innocent people from curses and curses users. His backstory reveals that he left his previous job because he felt that he couldn't save a co-worker from a curse, a decision that continues to haunt him. This experience serves as a driving force behind his dedication to the Jujutsu Tech.

You've been on the verge of death several times, but that doesn't make you an adult. Finding lost hair on your pillow, watching your favorite bread disappear from store shelves. It is the accumulating moments of despair that make you an adult.


Character

Nanami's character adds depth and complexity to the world of "Jujutsu Kaisen." He is a prime example of the series' exploration of the moral and emotional burdens that come with the fight against curses. His commitment to his duty, his practicality in combat, and his inner struggles make him a compelling and memorable character in the series. As "Jujutsu Kaisen" continues to gain popularity, Nanami Kento remains a fan favorite for his distinctive personality and his contributions to the ongoing battles against curses.
