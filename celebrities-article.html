Then and now: what is the difference between celebrities of the 90s and 00s from modern ones

To begin with, social media has created additional pressure on celebrities to constantly maintain a perfect image.
Millions of people follow them, so you need to think about how you look, how you speak and how you behave.
Cancel culture has also had a major impact on celebrities in the last few years.
It appeared precisely because of social networks, because it is based only on the power of public resonance. Many fall victim to the anger of fans: take, for example, the scandal with Johnny Depp and Amber Heard. It seems they were "cancelled" one by one

It's hard to say which celebrities have great taste and which ones have brilliant stylists. They always need to be stylishly dressed, especially when it comes to the red carpet.
Images from various awards (hello, Met Gala) are examined under a microscope by both ordinary social media users and professional stylists on their blogs. And, of course, there is quite a lot of criticism.
The fashion of the 2000s was so merciless that it is difficult to say whether anyone had stylists or stars dressed according to their soul. To imagine the looks that celebrities had back then, it's enough to remember Paris Hilton with her velor suits, low-waisted trousers, sequins and pink.

Of course, everyone knew that Gwyneth Paltrow was dating Ben Affleck - they went to events together. Particularly stubborn paparazzi could even photograph the celebrity right during the betrayal. And yet, hiding your love affairs was much easier then than it is now.

Scandals had already begun in the 2000s: Hollywood stars cheated on each other left and right and, it seems, everyone knew about it. Britney Spears and Justin Timberlake's breakup was all over the news, and Justin even wrote the song "Cry Me a River" which became a hit. Jude Law cheated on his wife with his nanny, Brad Pitt got divorced - in general, the 2000s delighted fans with drama.
A situation in which a media personality spoke out somehow incorrectly and no one paid attention is simply impossible. Remember the scandal with JK Rowling and her statements on X (formerly Twitter): even her name was removed from Fantastic Beasts.

Society began to come to some kind of political correctness only in the late nineties, so celebrities of that time could feel much freer during interviews. They weren't condemned for their words, but now it's hard to say whether it's good or bad.
In the 2000s, social networks began to appear, and stars were already faced with the fact that it was impossible to write anything on their Twitter - so they diligently cleaned up posts.
But they did not demand any kind of awareness from young celebrities. Everyone understood that a sixteen-year-old girl could say something strange - and this was forgiven. Now stars, regardless of age, are required to behave like adults, which is very sad.

The Internet has influenced the whole world and, of course, the lives of celebrities. Now it is very important for them to monitor their image and choose the right words so as not to spoil their image. This, of course, has its advantages: celebrities cannot bring destructive ideas into society, and social networks also help to quickly promote oneself. But being in the media was much easier in the nineties and 2000s, when people turned a blind eye to many things and did not demand “ideality.”
